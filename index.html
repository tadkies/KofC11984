Welcome

Brothers in faith, serving our parish

The Knights of Columbus are Catholic gentlemen committed to the exemplification of charity, unity, fraternity, patriotism, and defense of the priesthood. If you are a practical Catholic man living in or near southern Chesapeake and are interested in learning more or joining the Knights, we’d love to hear from you.

Chartered on May 25, 1997 as the first council on a Naval installation in the Commonwealth of Virginia, our council continues to serve the military Catholic community at Holy Family Chapel, NSA Northwest, and the Catholic community of St. Stephen, Martyr.

Our story
Our Namesake

Fr. Edward L. Richardson, SMA (1919–2008)

A priest of the Society of African Missions, Navy chaplain from the Korean War onward, and longtime Virginia State Chaplain of the Knights of Columbus. He was our council’s charter chaplain, and he lived by the motto “Build bridges with all people, never fences.”
