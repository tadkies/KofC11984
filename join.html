How to Join

Join a brotherhood of Catholic men who lead, protect, and serve.

Why become a Knight?

Men today need a band of brothers — other Catholic men who walk together on the journey of faith. As a Knight you’ll grow in your faith, serve your parish and community, support your family, and build lasting friendships.

Who can join?

Practical Catholic men age 18 and older. You do not need to be a parishioner of St. Stephen, Martyr — men from southern Chesapeake, NSA Northwest, and the surrounding area are welcome.
