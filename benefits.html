Fraternal Benefits

Protecting Catholic families has been part of the Knights of Columbus mission since its founding.

The Knights of Columbus was founded in 1882 by Blessed Michael McGivney, in part to protect the families of Catholic men who died or could not work. Today, members and their families can access a full line of insurance and retirement products from the Knights of Columbus, and investment services from Knights of Columbus Asset Advisors — all guided by Catholic values.
